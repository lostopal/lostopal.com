Method, Source & Licenses

Return to Nuncastra

The astronomical calculation happens in your browser. Lost Opal does not receive or store the date, time, coordinates, zodiac choice, or completed Nuncast. Place-name and ZIP-code searches use a compact local index built from GeoNames and U.S. Census Gazetteer data; the typed search remains on the device and is not sent to a geocoding service. Device location supplies coordinates through the browser’s own permission system, while directly entered coordinates bypass place-name search entirely. A small list of recently confirmed places may be kept only in this browser for convenient reuse.

When Lost Opal is installed to a device or revisited in a supporting browser, a service worker may cache the public site, Nuncastra software, requested place-index shards, ephemeris files, and Tarot artwork for offline use. This cache contains the same public files available from the website; it does not contain the dates, times, coordinates, certainty choices, or readings you enter.

What Is Being Calculated

Nuncastra uses Swiss Ephemeris for geocentric longitudes and apparent motion. Tropical is the Lost Opal default. Sidereal Lahiri and Fagan–Bradley apply the selected Swiss Ephemeris ayanamsa; these results remain a Lost Opal Tarot hybrid and are not represented as a complete Jyotish reading.

Every line now shows two different House layers. The Natural House is the Aries-first zodiac house carried by the sign: Aries is the 1st House, Taurus the 2nd, through Pisces as the 12th. It describes the sign’s native terrain. The Calculated House is derived from the selected time, latitude, and longitude using local Placidus cusps, with Equal houses used only when Placidus cusps are not mathematically usable. It describes where that celestial current is operating in the chosen chart. When the two numbers match, Nuncastra names the convergence instead of silently collapsing the layers.

The current body set contains the Sun through Pluto, Chiron, true North Node, derived South Node, Mean Black Moon Lilith, Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta, Ascendant, Midheaven, Vertex, and Lot of Fortune. The Lot uses ASC + Moon − Sun by day and ASC + Sun − Moon by night. The day/night decision is made from the Sun’s calculated position above or below the local horizon.

The Tarot Translation

Every line has three grammatical positions: celestial voice, zodiacal field, and exact ten-degree Golden Dawn decan. A body may bear more than one Lost Opal key without becoming more than one celestial body. Uranus is shown through The Fool, Death, and The Hanged Man; Pluto is shown through Death and Judgement. Their numerology uses the operative astrological key only rather than double-counting companion keys.

Myth and crystal tiles are compact correspondence aids rather than additional terms in the Tarot sentence. In Nuncastra they follow the actual celestial voice named by the row. In the shuffled Draw, a numbered Minor Arcana takes them from its Golden Dawn decan ruler. Each Major Arcana has a curated mythology list grounded in its configured planetary voice, including the ruler voice carried by zodiacal Trumps. Lost Opal exceptions remain explicit: Chiron belongs to The Hierophant, Neptune leads The Hanged Man, and outer-planet voices lead the mixed myth lists for Death and The Star. Constellation myth names remain with the zodiacal field; Taurus names Europa and Zeus as the Bull. Myth tiles provide names for further study, while Crystals show one primary and two commonly related correspondences.

In the shuffled Draw, Numerology and Tree of Life are optional perspectives rather than ordinary correspondence tiles. Their closed tabs show the reduced numerological current and the card’s path or Sephirah for quick reference; opening them reveals the reduction, related Tarot keys, and Qabalistic placement. Lost Opal’s court sequence assigns Kings to 2 and Chokmah, Queens to 3 and Binah, Knights to 9 and Yesod, and Pages to 10 and Malkuth. Pages begin at Ten and resolve to One as the beginner current. The Wheel and the four numbered Tens retain Ten as their completed Tarot current, and compound-card reductions stop at Ten rather than forcing it into One. Zero remains the distinct current of The Fool.

The smaller esoteric title displayed beneath each Tarot card name follows the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn nomenclature recorded in Book T. Numbered Minor Arcana therefore appear as the appropriate “Lord of…” title, while the Trumps and court cards retain forms such as Daughter, Child, Magus, Princess, Queen, or Prince where the source tradition calls for them.

Sign rulership is shown as a contextual lens rather than a fourth term in the Tarot sentence or numerology equation. The sign-card position can be turned from its zodiacal Major Arcana to the Tarot card of its traditional ruler. Nuncastra uses the traditional seven-planet rulership scheme that underlies the Golden Dawn decans; Pluto, Uranus, and Neptune are acknowledged as modern co-rulers of Scorpio, Aquarius, and Pisces without displacing Mars, Saturn, and Jupiter in the operative layer. In this Lost Opal system, the Moon is represented by The Moon.

The North Node uses The Moon upright and the South Node uses The Moon reversed, with separate node seals. Chiron uses The Hierophant as a Lost Opal correspondence. Bodies and points without a settled Tarot mapping keep their own names, use small pre-rendered placeholder cards, and carry a compact warning that explains the correspondence is still in development.

The Tree of Life Chamber

The optional study chamber draws the ten Sephiroth, Da’ath as an unnumbered threshold, and all twenty-two Golden Dawn paths. The entity key paths, zodiacal key path, Minor Arcana Sephirah, and Qabalistic world are illuminated only after the user chooses to enter. Lost Opal overlays are identified separately: Uranus occupies Da’ath; Pluto occupies Kether and is represented there by Judgement.

Where the Written Readings Come From

The meanings and “Ground it” guidance are a hand-authored Lost Opal interpretation library bundled directly with Nuncastra. The browser selects and combines the matching celestial, zodiacal, and decan entries after calculation. The page does not call an AI interpretation service or send a chart to a remote reading server.

The ordinary reading intentionally uses accessible Tarot language. Deeper path, Hebrew-letter, and Sephirotic teachings remain behind the Tree of Life entry rather than being forced into every reading.

Print Treatments

Ink-friendly is the default physical-print treatment and removes full-page color fields. Keepsake PDF intentionally retains a parchment-toned presentation. Card artwork can independently be printed in color, grayscale, or as names only.

Image Delivery

Public artwork is served as right-sized WebP imagery. Tarot cards below the current viewport load only as they are approached, decode away from the main rendering path, and are prepared before the browser opens the print dialog so a faster screen reading does not produce an incomplete PDF.
