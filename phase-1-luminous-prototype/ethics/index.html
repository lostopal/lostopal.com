Ethics & boundaries

What you can expect from me.

A reading may hold mystery. The terms never should. This page states what I will do, what I will not do, and what always remains yours.

Read the code Reader’s Oath Return to readings

The short version

You remain in charge of your life and your choices.

Put simply:

  • No scare tactics.
  • No pretending I can read someone else’s mind.
  • No professional advice dressed up as revelation.
  • No pressure to pay, donate, return, or believe what I believe.

The code of practice

The promises behind the work.

These standards apply whether we meet privately, in public, at an event, or through a live stream.

  1. 01

    Adults only

    I currently read only for people who are 18 or older. I do not make exceptions for parental permission, events, or online requests.

  2. 02

    Consent is active and may be withdrawn

    You choose the question, what you disclose, and what is off limits. You may pause, redirect, or end a reading at any time. I may also decline or stop a reading that I cannot conduct responsibly.

  3. 03

    Your agency remains yours

    Cards and charts can illuminate patterns, possibilities, past influences, and possible futures; they do not issue commands or remove your responsibility to choose. I will not encourage dependence on me or on repeated readings.

  4. 04

    Privacy depends on the setting

    Private readings are treated as private and are not recorded, shared, or used as content without your explicit permission. Public and live-stream readings are public by nature; I will say so clearly and avoid unnecessary identifying details.

  5. 05

    No spying on absent people

    I will not claim access to another person’s private thoughts, secrets, or hidden actions. We can examine your experience of a relationship, the pattern between you, and the choices available to you.

  6. 06

    Clear professional limits

    A reading is not medical care, mental-health treatment, legal counsel, financial planning, or emergency support. I do not diagnose, prescribe, or replace qualified professionals. When a question belongs with one, I will say so.

  7. 07

    No fear tactics or manufactured emergencies

    I will not tell you that you are cursed, spiritually contaminated, doomed, or required to pay for a remedy. I will not use frightening language, certainty, or urgency to sell another service.

  8. 08

    Money is discussed honestly

    Public and live-stream readings are free; donations are optional and create no debt or obligation. Any private reading or event price will be agreed in advance. There will be no surprise spiritual fees.

  9. 09

    Your beliefs and identity are respected

    You do not need to share my spiritual language or accept a supernatural explanation. I will not proselytize, shame, or use a reading to impose my theology, philosophy, politics, or personal values.

  10. 10

    Professional conduct at the table

    I will not exploit vulnerability, pursue romantic or sexual attention through the reading relationship, or tolerate harassment and abuse. Every person is owed dignity; every reader is allowed a safe boundary.

  11. 11

    Recommendations and affiliations are disclosed

    If a link, product, sponsor, or recommendation may compensate Lost Opal, I will label that relationship clearly. A commercial relationship will never be disguised as spiritual necessity.

  12. 12

    I remain accountable to the work

    I will distinguish tradition from personal interpretation, admit uncertainty, correct meaningful errors, and continue learning. If I cannot serve a question honestly or competently, I will not pretend that I can.

Reader’s Oath

The promise I make when you take your seat.

When you take your seat, I promise to:

  • Listen before I interpret.
  • Tell you what I see, and say so when I am unsure.
  • Keep your choices in your hands.
  • Never use fear, secrecy, or urgency to make you depend on me.
  • Respect your privacy, your boundaries, your beliefs, and your right to stop.
  • Step back when a question belongs with a doctor, therapist, lawyer, financial adviser, or emergency service.
  • Be honest about money, referrals, affiliate links, and my mistakes.
  • Use the symbols to help you hear yourself more clearly, not to speak over you.
  • Serve the Sacred Fire within humanity by helping you leave with more clarity and more agency.

Influence & further reading

A note on an important influence.

Anthony Louis’s chapter on Tarot ethics in Llewellyn’s Complete Book of Tarot: A Comprehensive Guide helped prompt a fuller examination of my own standards. The code and oath on this page are original to Lost Opal; they credit that influence without reproducing the book’s oath or prose.
