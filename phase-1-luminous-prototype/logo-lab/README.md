# Lost Opal solar-seal construction lab

This is a separate, local-only logo construction proof inside the luminous
Phase 1 prototype. It is not an adopted logo, a replacement homepage, or a
publishing asset.

Concept 07 uses the existing Lost Opal crest artwork as an untouched ancestral
base. Its overlays are placement studies only; they do not alter the source
logo file.

Open <http://localhost:8082/logo-lab/> while the luminous preview server is
running.

Material Render V7 is saved at `../assets/lost-opal-logo-render-v7.png`. It keeps
the calmer V5 filigree silhouette, then swaps the seal's material hierarchy. The
twelve separate zodiac seats become one continuous black-opal collar. The large
inner field becomes the same calm textured black used behind the wordmark, and
the six planet cabochons and connectors are removed. A very small opal locator
remains at center beneath the exact browser-built Sun. Earlier renders remain
untouched as iteration history.

The live V7 review applies exact raised-gold zodiac and planet glyphs as separate
browser-rendered layers, constructs the small central Sun in CSS with an opal
point, and sets the exact `Tarot & Astrology` descriptor in the lower plaque.
Aries begins at nine o'clock and the zodiac proceeds counterclockwise. All twelve
zodiac coordinates now come from one mathematically even ring, and each symbol
has its own optical correction based on the visible glyph rather than its font
box. The six planets use the same method on a true hexagonal orbit. The
descriptor uses the locally bundled UnifrakturCook blackletter face to relate
directly to the main `Lost Opal` wordmark. Its OFL license is stored beside the
font under `../assets/fonts/`.

## What this proof tests

- the inner black medallion as a quiet solar field, with one small gold-ringed
  opal-accented Sun at its exact center;
- the other six classical planets as simple raised-gold glyphs on the black
  field, without individual stones, sockets, or connector lines;
- Aries at nine o'clock with all twelve zodiac signs proceeding
  counterclockwise on one continuous black-opal collar;
- the original `Lost Opal` blackletter plaque as the dominant identity, with
  `Tarot & Astrology` replacing the former Tarot-only descriptor;
- Hermetic meaning carried implicitly by the solar center, sevenfold planetary
  system, zodiac order, infinity crowning the crest, and gilded structure rather than another
  written descriptor or symbol cluster;
- core mark, standard lockup, and ceremonial-seal reductions;
- a public balanced mode and a restrained personal-triad mode.

The personal triad is Sun + Mercury + Moon: the solar/Tiphareth center, Mercury
as the ruler of Gemini, and the Moon as the ruler of Cancer. It changes visual
emphasis only. It does not move a planet, alter the zodiac order, or imply that
the other planetary forces are absent.

## Deliberately unfinished

- The original crest image is not the finished redesign; it is the lineage
  reference beneath the placement proof.
- The current gold, black-inlay, and opal treatments are material-placement tests, not final
  rendering or production artwork.
- The descriptor wording remains removable and can change without rebuilding
  the core mark.

The symbolic geometry, material balance, and glyph audit must be approved before
the production logo is redrawn and exported.
