# Planetary Cabochons — v2

The v2 family replaces the single repeated opal field from v1 with a distinct
stone body for every planetary glyph while preserving the approved centered
glyphs, uninterrupted stud-free gold bezel, transparent 512 × 512 canvas, and
shared visual footprint.

## Planetary material signatures

- Moon — midnight blue/violet black opal; cool pearl/lavender white opal
- Mercury — teal/emerald pinfire black opal; mint/cyan pinfire white opal
- Mars — ember-red/copper black opal; coral/peach white opal
- Saturn — restrained steel-blue/gold black opal; champagne/gray-blue white opal
- Jupiter — broad royal-blue/emerald black opal; sky/seafoam/gold white opal
- Venus — rose/lilac/teal black opal; blush/lilac/mint white opal
- Sun — amber/orange/blue-fire black opal; warm cream/champagne white opal

## Final assets

- `black-opal/`
- `white-opal/`
- `white-opal-outlined/`
- `planetary-cabochons-v2-contact-sheet.png`

The outlined white family uses the same unique planetary stone bodies as the
plain white family and retains the fine charcoal glyph keyline for small-scale
legibility. Source textures generated with the built-in image-generation tool
are retained under `textures/` so the family can be rebuilt non-destructively.
