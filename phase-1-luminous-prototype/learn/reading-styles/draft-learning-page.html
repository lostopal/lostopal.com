Before the cards are placed

A spread is scaffolding—not a cage.

A layout helps us hold several parts of a question at once. A position might name an influence, tension, resource, choice, relationship, or possibility. The structure gives us somewhere to begin; the living conversation determines where we go.

I may use a known spread, reshape it, draw only one or two cards, or build a custom spread live. The goal is not to force your life into a diagram. The goal is to make the pattern easier to see.

Choosing the shape

The question comes first.

You do not need to arrive knowing the name of a spread. We can choose the structure together once I understand what you want to explore.

  1. 01

    Name what is alive.

    Love, work, growth, direction, uncertainty, celebration, or something harder to name.

  2. 02

    Set the boundary.

    Decide what belongs in the conversation and what does not.

  3. 03

    Choose the structure.

    Use a familiar layout, a direct draw, or a custom pattern.

  4. 04

    Let it breathe.

    Follow useful connections without treating the diagram as a script.

I read patterns and possibilities, not fixed fate. I do not claim to read another person’s mind or guarantee an outcome.

Beyond the spread
